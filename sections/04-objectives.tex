\subsection{Objectives}



Our objectives focus on solving real problems for real people:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{To develop a robust indoor telepresence robot with autonomous navigation} %This core objective is to create a functional platform that enables healthcare professionals to conduct remote consultations, perform wellness checks, and provide medication reminders. A secure connection is ensured with the help of well-known services like Google Meet. This provides a video call with unnoticeable latency and secure communication. The system will allow patients to receive timely medical attention without leaving their homes or hospital beds, reducing the stress and cost of travel while improving compliance with treatment plans.

    \item \textbf{To engineer a safety-aware navigation system for crowded areas} %Recognizing that even the most advanced autonomous system faces challenges in dynamic human environments like hospitals, this objective prioritizes safety above all else. The system will be designed to operate reliably and cautiously in busy corridors. If a moving obstacle is detected, the robot will intelligently avoid being in its path by leveraging ROS-based route re-planning and dynamic obstacle avoidance algorithms, ensuring the safety and comfort of patients, staff, and visitors.

    \item \textbf{To design a clean, user-friendly system with an intuitive graphical user interface (GUI)} %The system is designed for ease of use by the clinician, not the patient. Elderly patients, disabled individuals, and children will not be required to interact with complex controls on the system. Instead, actions on the screen, such as initiating a call or displaying information, will be activated remotely by the doctor or nurse, ensuring a seamless, accessible, and stress-free experience for the patient.

    \item \textbf{Secure, real-time A/V consult suitable for bedside use. }

    \item \textbf{A pilotable demo scenario (nurse station → Bed X → short consult → return) } %A container with a secure lid will be mounted on the robot to facilitate the transport of essential items. This feature is intended for carrying medications from the pharmacy to a patient's room, delivering lab samples, or transporting small medical supplies. This automates routine logistical tasks, freeing up valuable nursing time that can be redirected toward more complex and direct patient care.
\end{itemize}
